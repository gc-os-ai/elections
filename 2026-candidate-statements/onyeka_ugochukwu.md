I am a firm believer in open source. Over the last five years of my life, I have devoted time and other resources to help advance the open source ideology, and to demonstrate how open source creates value, ensures equity and engenders inclusion of persons that erstwhile would have been excluded from benefits accruing to software development. 

I have served in GC.OS as one of the founding members/advisors. I believe that I have what it takes to continue to contribute to the steering the GC.OS organization in line with its founders' ideals, vision and mission. I will continue to bring my passion, skills and other resources to bear as we work towards ensuring that no one is left behind or denied benefits of open source software, either due to pecuniary interests of mega-controlling organizations or governments, or economic disadvantage occasioned by lack of finance and any other inhibitors.
