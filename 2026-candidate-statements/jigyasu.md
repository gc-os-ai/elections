For the last two years, I’ve been actively involved in open-source software development, contributing code, improving documentation and infrastructure, and working with maintainers and contributors. Through these experiences, I’ve seen both the opportunities that open source creates and some of the challenges contributors face, especially when it comes to finding ways to get involved and navigating large communities.

I am Jigyasu, a core developer of sktime and a council observer at GC.OS. I started my journey as an sktime mentee, then went on to work on benchmarking and dataset infrastructure as a European Summer of Code mentee, and now contribute to the project as a core developer. I have also given a talk on sktime at IndiaFOSS 2025 in Bengaluru and have closely followed the GC.OS council and its decisions as an observer.

If elected, I would focus on making the open-source ecosystem more welcoming to new contributors. When I talk about making the ecosystem more welcoming, I also mean it in a technical sense: ensuring that projects under the GC.OS umbrella remain well documented, including the internal tools and utilities that are often known only to a small group of developers. Good documentation and accessible project infrastructure can significantly lower the barrier to entry for new contributors.

I would also work with the elected council to help projects navigate the growing use of AI in open-source development. I believe we should develop policies that protect the sustainability, quality, and integrity of our projects without being dismissive of enthusiastic contributors who want to use new tools. The goal should be to find a balance that encourages innovation while ensuring that AI-assisted contributions remain responsible and beneficial to the projects and their communities.

Open source has given me the opportunity to learn, collaborate, and contribute alongside people from around the world. I would be grateful for the opportunity to give back by representing the community and helping build an open-source ecosystem that is more sustainable, inclusive, and accessible to everyone.
