# Candidate Statement for Marc Rovira

## About me

For those who don't know me, I'm Marc Rovira - `marrov` on GitHub. I work as a Senior Data Scientist, live in Stockholm (Sweden), and have been an active member of the GC.OS and `sktime` community. I moved from an avid user of `sktime`, to active community member, then member of the first elected community council at `sktime`, and now part of the transitional council for GC.OS.

## Contribution history

Throughout my time in these projects I have contributed to the community and tech in several ways. These are some of my most recent contributions:

- Continued community council role since elected in 2024 - overseen the creation of the non-profit organization, decided on election procedures, member of the CoC, brought in new projects (e.g. `pygam`), contributed to PR and branding discussions, and have had a minor role in the development of sktime ES.
- ESOC project: I sponsored (through my employer's affiliation) and mentored for the "Causal Forecasting" ESOC project, which extended both `pgmpy` and `sktime` capabilities within causal estimation.
- Although historically I have contributed more to the community, I have increase my tech contributions in `sktime`. Apart from code reviews and minor fixes, I recently contributed `MCRecursiveProbaReductionForecaster` which is an internally (at my employer) meta algorithm for distributional probabilistic forecasting with ML. Though unmerged, this has probably been my single largest technical contribution to date. 

## Future vision

I believe GC.OS should continue to focus on implementing its vision of a democratic, transparent, and community-led open-source technological landscape. If re-elected, I will continue to focus on community engagement and developing the transparent organizational foundations required to achieve the mission of GC.OS.
